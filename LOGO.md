# Logo

LOGO.png is the source for the platform icons. Regenerate PNG/ICO/ICNS assets with `python tools/build-icons.py` (Pillow required).

Initial image-generation prompt:

> Create a distinctive app logo for an AI coding workspace application named Vibe Harder. A single bold abstract VH monogram combined subtly with a terminal prompt, readable at 24 pixels. Modern, confident, playful but professional developer-tool identity. Flat crisp vector-like geometry, lavender and warm electric coral on a deep charcoal rounded-square tile, large simple silhouette, generous safe margins. No gradients, no text other than the abstract VH shape, no tiny details, no mockup. Square 1024 by 1024 PNG, transparent outside the rounded square. This is the application's production logo.
