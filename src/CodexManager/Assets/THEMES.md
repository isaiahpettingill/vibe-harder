Theme colors are adapted to Vibe Harder's shared UI and terminal palette:

- Quiet Light and Monokai Dimmed: [microsoft/vscode](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/tree/main/extensions) (MIT).
- Tokyo Night, Storm, and Light: [tokyo-night/tokyo-night-vscode-theme](https://github.com/tokyo-night/tokyo-night-vscode-theme) (MIT).
- Catppuccin Latte: [catppuccin/palette](https://github.com/catppuccin/palette) (MIT).
- Nord Frost and Nord Aurora are Vibe Harder variants of the [Nord palette](https://github.com/nordtheme/nord) (MIT), emphasizing its Frost and Aurora accent colors respectively.

UI surfaces and readable secondary text are adapted to this app; these are palette adaptations rather than complete VS Code syntax themes.
