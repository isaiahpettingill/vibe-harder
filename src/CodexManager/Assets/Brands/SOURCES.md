# Provider marks

Downloaded 2026-09-11. Used to identify the provider of a chat; no affiliation is implied.

- OpenAI: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/master/packages/static-svg/icons/openai.svg
- Anthropic: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/master/packages/static-svg/icons/anthropic.svg
- OpenCode: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anomalyco/opencode/dev/packages/console/app/src/asset/brand/opencode-logo-dark.svg

OpenAI and Anthropic SVGs are from Lobe Icons (MIT, accompanying LICENSE-LobeHub.txt). OpenCode's SVG is from its official brand assets: https://opencode.ai/brand. The marks remain the property of their owners; asset licensing does not grant trademark rights.

The separate Vibe Harder application icon is an original geometric workspace/chat design, not a provider logo.

- Pi: official monochrome mark from https://pi.dev/favicon.svg (downloaded 2026-09-15), used to identify Pi. Paths are unchanged; fills use currentColor to match the app theme.
- Dirac: original vector drawing of a cursive lowercase Greek delta, requested for this app; not an official brand asset.

- Cline: official filled logo from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cline/cline/main/apps/cline-hub/src/webview/public/cline-logo-filled.svg (downloaded 2026-09-15). Original path; fill uses currentColor.
